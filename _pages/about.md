## Research focus

My research connects physics, mathematics, and computer science across three complementary areas:

- **Quantum computing and quantum machine learning:** algorithms, variational methods, quantum neural networks, and quantum-classical models.
- **Secure and responsible AI:** quantum cryptography, adversarial robustness, and machine learning for complex information systems.
- **Scientific computing and education:** reproducible computational methods, data science, visualization, and training for emerging technologies.

## Initiatives and collaboration

I build programs that connect research, education, and access to emerging technologies:

- **[QMexico](http://qmexico.org/){:target="_blank"}:** quantum science and education in Latin America.
- **[Qaldas](http://qaldas.com){:target="_blank"}:** research, software, consulting, and advanced data analytics.
- **[Quaker-ECE](https://quaker-ece.cs.earlham.edu/){:target="_blank"}:** interdisciplinary training in quantum computing and emerging technologies.
